Terms and privacy

The service

This is a video meeting service for up to four people at a time. A host account costs , charged monthly through Stripe, and can be cancelled at any time from the dashboard; access continues until the end of the period you've paid for. Joining a meeting by link is free.

What we keep

Your name, email address and a scrambled (hashed) copy of your password; the meetings you schedule and the email addresses you invite; and billing status. Card details go straight to Stripe and never reach us. We keep records you have asked us to keep until you delete them or your account.

What we don't keep

Video, audio, screen shares and chat are not recorded or stored. Calls are relayed through Cloudflare's TURN servers, so participants don't see each other's IP addresses. Chat messages exist only while the meeting is open.

Emails

We email you to confirm your address, reset your password and send the invitations you ask us to send. Invitations are sent only when a host enters the address. We don't send marketing.

Using it responsibly

Don't use the service for anything unlawful or to harass people. Only record a meeting with everyone's consent. We may suspend accounts that abuse the service.

Your rights

You can delete your account and all of its data yourself from the dashboard. Contact the site owner for any other request about your data.

Service providers: Cloudflare (hosting, video relay, email) and Stripe (payments).
